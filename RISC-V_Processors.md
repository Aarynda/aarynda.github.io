## Abstract
Following the completion of my undergraduate computer architecture course, my partner, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/arjun-siderys/">Arjun Siderys</a>, and I have decided to re-complete and continuously improve our RISC-V processor from the course.

## Current Stage of Implementation
The current Verilog implementation of the processor is a simple single-cycle CPU, and current efforts are focused on verifying the processor functionality for RV32I before continuing on the roadmap.
